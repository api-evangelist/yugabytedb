On June 11, the first four FIFA World Cup 2026 matches will kick off in a six-hour window, with tens of millions of fans logging in daily to the same applications to watch games and place bets across the expanded 104-game, 39-day tournament. The post uses this high-concurrency scenario to illustrate why multi-agent architectures are necessary for applications that must scale to meet sudden, massive demand. YugabyteDB's distributed SQL design is presented as the data layer that enables such agent strategies to operate reliably at scale.
