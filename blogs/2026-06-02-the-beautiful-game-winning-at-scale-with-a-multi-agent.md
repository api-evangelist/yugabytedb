During major live sporting events, peak traffic reaches unprecedented levels, and customers expect a flawless in-the-moment experience. The right data infrastructure separates the platforms that win from the ones that fail. This blog uses the FIFA World Cup as an example and explores why successful platforms are building multi-agent systems on a unified data infrastructure to handle seasonal and event-driven traffic spikes.
